What matters first

Crimson Harvest runs from October 6 to November 3, 2026. The new wrinkle is Pochven pressure: Blood Raiders and Order of St. Tetrimon are fighting around Triglavian space, with Criterion Ciphers opening solo Criterion of Fitness sites.

Duration

Oct 6 to Nov 3

Daily login rewards, Agency challenges, combat/data sites, token traders, and late-month escalation rewards.

Faction choice

Pick for rewards, not access

Your side changes which Agency tasks score and which SKIN line you earn. You can still shoot and loot both sides.

Risk modifier

Loot fairy goes feral

Pods can drop implants at 50%, and ships outside highsec drop 90% of their loot during the event. Treat lowsec and Pochven like bait has a salary.

The simple loop

If you just want to undock and make progress, this is the shortest path.

Choose the opposite targets for Agency points

Blood Raider alignment wants Tetrimon kills/hacks. Tetrimon alignment wants Blood Raider kills/hacks. Loot is fair game either way.

Run standard combat or data sites

Combat sites are the steady path for samples, boss drops, and possible rift spawns. Data sites are faster if you can scan, hack, and dodge hunters.

Kill optional rift spawns when your ship can handle them

After clearing rooms in Crimson Gauntlet or Tetrimon Base, an Abyssal rift may spawn Rogue Drone, Sleeper, or Triglavian extras. The boss can drop Mutated Blood Samples and Criterion Ciphers.

Use ciphers for Criterion of Fitness

These are solo timed Triglavian trials. Faster wave clears raise difficulty and reward quality. Bring serious tank, cap planning, and damage projection.

Spend samples at the right trader

Most cosmetics and SKINR items are available everywhere. Faction/officer toys are trader-specific. Check market depth before chasing spreadsheet top spots.

Where to go

Use the Agency and Probe Scanner. Combat sites are the brawl lane; data sites are the travel-light lane; Hollow Outbreak and Criterion sites are the 2026 advanced lane.

With what

These are role guidelines, not sacred fits. The event rewards repeatability more than elegance.

Combat starter

Battlecruiser or HAC

  • Prioritize EM/Thermal tank for Blood Raider and Tetrimon rooms.
  • Bring a web or application rigs if your guns hate frigates.
  • Cap booster beats dying nobly to neut pressure.
  • Salvage after if you are not racing site spawns.
Data runner

Cheap scanner

  • Data Analyzer, probe launcher, MWD, cloak if trained.
  • Fit for align time and escape, not killmail beauty.
  • Use cargo scanner to skip bad cans when traffic is hot.
  • Lowsec hubs are not AFK archaeology. Keep d-scan open.
Criterion runner

Marauder or serious brawler

  • Timed waves reward high applied DPS and staying power.
  • Expect Triglavian ramping damage and possible heavy neut bosses.
  • Clear speed controls the difficulty modifier and reward score.
  • Do first attempts in a hull whose loss will not ruin your week.
Highsec is safer, not safe. The event pushes valuable loot into predictable locations. Outside highsec, the 90% loot drop rule makes every shiny fit look like a public loot pinata.

Criterion of Fitness

Use a Criterion Cipher from rift boss drops to enter. The gate closes after one ship passes, so this is a solo performance check.

How scoring works

You fight three Triglavian waves. Each wave has a time threshold. Faster clears add more fitness and raise the next wave's challenge. Max score is 15, which controls the final cache quality.

Practical read

  • If wave one feels spicy, do not chase the highest score.
  • Elite Hospodar-style pressure becomes more likely as score rises.
  • Boss quality scales: Drekavac, Leshak, or the nasty Babaroga tier at top score.
  • The reward cache is the reason to push; survival is the reason to be honest.
